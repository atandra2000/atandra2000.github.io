Work

Nov 2022 to now

Independent ML engineering. Every result below was produced on hardware I configured and monitored.

LLaMA-3-Lite · memory engineering

A 515M-parameter LLaMA-3-style transformer, cut from 92 GB to 20 GB peak training memory on a single A100 80GB. Gradient checkpointing, chunked cross-entropy taking logits from 50 GB to 0.3 GB, disk-backed token caching taking RAM from 112 GB to 1 MB, BF16, Flash-Attn 2, channels_last and fused AdamW.

HyMo · hybrid LLM

434M active and 1.13B stored parameters, fusing Gated Delta Net linear attention with Multi-Head Latent Attention in a 3:1 ratio, plus Asymmetric MoE and Multi-Token Prediction. Pre-trained from scratch on 30B tokens with a hand-written Triton GDN kernel and FSDP-2.

Stable Diffusion 1.x · from random init

An 860M-parameter UNet trained on 2× RTX 5090 across a 7-phase curriculum, 1.3M+ images from LAION-Aesthetic through DiffusionDB, JourneyDB, VGGFace2 and COCO. Best loss 0.0947 at epoch 16; the epoch-42 checkpoint is on HuggingFace.

Autonomous ML Research Engineer · agentic platform

15 phases, 20 agents and 61 typed tools that carry an arXiv paper through analysis, planning, code patches, training runs, statistical evaluation and a written report. Provider-agnostic LLM routing with self-repair.

Frontier reproductions

CyberLM, a 4.55B / 1.34B-active sovereign cyber MoE with an auditable tool harness and GRPO. A faithful DeepSeek-V3 with the MLA absorption trick and AuxLossFreeGate MoE. GPT-OSS with alternating sliding and full attention plus YaRN at 128K. Mamba-3 with a complex64 SSD and 50% smaller state.
